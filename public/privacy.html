[vault] Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

[vault] is a small, private, invite-only app for storing and working on music projects. It has no ads and does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.

[vault] is open source. Anyone can read exactly what it does with your data: github.com/waaqef/vault.

What [vault] stores

  • Your account: your username, a one-way hash of your password (never the password itself) and your profile picture, if you add one.
  • Your library: the names, notes, tags, BPM, key and folder structure of your projects and tracks.
  • Share links you make: the names listeners type in, what they played and for how long, and any comments they leave.

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, your music and cover art are stored in your own Google Drive, in a folder called [vault]. [vault] asks only for the drive.file permission, so it can see and change only the files it created itself. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.

To keep your Drive connected, [vault] stores an access token from Google, encrypted. It uses that token only to upload, play, download and delete your [vault] files, and to show how much Drive space you have used. It also reads the email address of your Google account so that Settings can show which account is connected.

[vault]'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never used for advertising, never sold, never transferred to anyone else, and never read by a person, unless you ask for help with a problem or the law requires it.

Who can see your things

Only you. Other [vault] users can't see your projects or files. The person who runs [vault] can see each account's username, picture, track count and storage used, but not your files. Anyone with a share link you made can play what that link shares, until you turn the link off.

Where it's kept

Account and library information is stored on Cloudflare. Files belonging to the admin account are stored on Backblaze B2, and every other account's files are stored in that person's own Google Drive. Songs you play may also be saved in your browser so they don't have to download again. These are removed when you log out.

Deleting your data

You can delete any track or project at any time; it goes to Recently deleted and is removed for good after 30 days, or straight away if you empty it. To delete your whole account, ask the person who invited you. This removes your account, your library and the files [vault] created in your Drive. You can also remove [vault]'s access to your Drive at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.

Contact

For questions or to delete your data, contact the person who invited you to [vault].
