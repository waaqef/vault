[your data]

Where your music is stored

[vault] is invite-only. When you sign up, you connect Google Drive, and everything you upload is stored there, in your space and under your control. Here's exactly what [vault] can and can't do with that access.

[what the access is used for]
  • Upload the songs and cover art you add
  • Play, download and edit those files for you
  • Delete them when you delete them in [vault]
  • Show how much Drive space you have left, and which account is connected
  • Never sold, never used for ads, never shared
  • Can't see any file it didn't create

Remove access any time from your Google Account permissions. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.
