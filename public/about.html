[vault]

A private, invite-only app for storing and working on music projects, inspired by [untitled].

Sign in

What [vault] does

  • Store your music projects. Keep beats, demos and ideas in projects and folders, with cover art, notes, tags, BPM and key.
  • Play everything back. Waveforms, a queue, a full-screen player, and songs kept on your device so they play offline.
  • Edit the audio. Varispeed, pitch, EQ, compressor, gain, trims and fades, saved as a new version of the track while older versions stay available.
  • Share with a link. Share a project or a track. Listeners can play it and leave comments at a moment in the track, and you can see who listened.
  • Upload from anywhere. Upload from a phone or computer, or straight from the iPhone share sheet.

Accounts

[vault] is invite-only. Each person gets an invite code from the admin, then signs up with a username, password and optional profile picture. Nobody can see anyone else's projects or files.

Why [vault] asks for access to Google Drive

When you sign up, [vault] asks you to connect your Google Drive. Your music is stored in your own Drive, in a folder called [vault], so it uses your own storage and stays under your control.

[vault] requests only the drive.file permission. That lets it create, read and delete only the files it uploaded itself. It cannot see or change anything else in your Drive. It uses this access only to:

  • upload the songs and cover art you add to [vault],
  • play, download and edit those files for you,
  • delete them when you delete them in [vault],
  • show how much Drive storage you have left, and which Google account is connected.

Your Google data is never sold, never used for ads, and never shared with anyone else. You can remove [vault]'s access at any time from your Google Account permissions. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Open source

[vault]'s code is public, so anyone can check exactly what it does: github.com/waaqef/vault.
